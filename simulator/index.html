Control rules (v3)

  • Y always selects. B always goes back (hold for home). X is the app's own action, and each screen says what it does.
  • L2 is the microphone. Hold it to dictate into the current screen; on screens with nothing to dictate, it talks to Hermes. In Voice memo it's tap to start, tap to stop.
  • L in Hermes turns on quiet wait: the screen goes blank until Hermes' reply arrives.
  • − locks the controller and turns the screen off; only − unlocks it. Use it before the controller goes in your pocket. Hold − mutes or unmutes passive listening (the daily recording and voice matching). While locked, − only unlocks, so a pocket press can't flip it. It starts on; Home shows LISTEN OFF while muted.
  • ★ apps · ♥ people heard in the last 10 minutes (◀▶ switches person, ▼ shows more) · R notifications · R2 play/pause (hold for volume and skip) · A now playing.
  • Voice call lives in Hermes' X menu, not on a global button. Morning report is its own app. The app order can be changed in Settings.
